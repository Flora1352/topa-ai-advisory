About Flora

I'm Flora Bahari, an interior designer, business professional, master of ceremonies (MC), and singer based in the Greater Toronto Area.

I founded Flora Staging and Design in 2012, working with homeowners on interior design and home staging. At Eskan Canada Building Group, I help clients make informed renovation decisions through design consultation, material selection, and product solutions.

My background also includes sales, client relations, and day-to-day management in the condominium accommodation business.

I completed the AI for Business course at CAMA College and am currently studying AI Advisory, with a focus on practical applications of AI in business.

I previously hosted a live radio show on Radio Arina. Through broadcasting, live event hosting, and singing, I bring a strong interest in voice, storytelling, and connecting with an audience.

My personal brand brings together AI advisory, design, business experience, and communication. I value thoughtful work, clear language, and solutions grounded in people's actual needs.

How we work — the TOPA method

Every engagement follows the same six steps, taking an idea from paper to flight — the journey of TOPA's white bird, from origami to a real bird in the air.

  1. 01

    Discover

    We learn your business first — your goals, your people, and how work actually gets done day to day.

  2. 02

    Diagnose

    We pinpoint where AI will genuinely help — and where it won't — so effort goes only where it counts.

  3. 03

    Design

    We shape the solution around your team, your tools, and your clients — comparing where you are now with where you plan to be.

  4. 04

    Roadmap

    You receive a clear, step-by-step plan: what happens, in what order, and why.

  5. 05

    Deploy

    The solution goes into daily use, with your people supported as it lands.

  6. 06

    Measure

    We check the results against the goal — and refine whatever needs refining.
